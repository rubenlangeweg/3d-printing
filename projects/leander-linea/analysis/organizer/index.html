Assumptions and how to read the result

Uniform, solid, linear-elastic PLA reference: E = 2.06 GPa and ν = 0.35. PLA reference specimens used 100% infill and were annealed and dried at 55 °C for 8 hours; ordinary prints may differ. Print layers, infill, creep, imperfect contact and the wooden table’s movement are not modelled. A fully fixed lip can be stiffer than the real attachment.

The added contents load is distributed over the inner floor, representing low contents. It does not simulate a tall bottle pressing against the walls or a local point load.

Red means the top of the displayed colour legend, not danger or failure. Surface stress colours average adjacent elements; peak element stresses are sensitive to the mesh and idealized constraint. No failure limit is applied.

Each selected case includes self-weight and its stated contents load. The page displays the complete solved field; it does not multiply a gravity-loaded result by the contents mass. Magnification changes the picture only. Animation cycles the static field and is not a vibration or impact simulation.
