Assumptions and numerical checks
  • Uniform solid PLA reference: E = 2.06 GPa, Poisson ratio 0.35, density 1.24 g/cm³. Print layers, infill and creep are not represented.
  • Small-displacement, frictionless, compression-only contact against a rigid rail. Your confirmed fit supports the snug-fit assumption; exact zero clearance is idealized.
  • Added load is distributed over a 30 × 30 mm patch, 130 mm from the rail origin; the corner case is offset 65 mm across the width.
  • Identical contact nodes, normals, load patches and lattice are checked for both designs. Each case must pass force/moment equilibrium and solver convergence checks.
  • This is a 2 mm comparison. The earlier V3 finer-grid check changed front movement by about 6%; V4 has not received an independent finer-grid check. Local peak stresses are not used as failure limits.
  • Wood/frame bending and whole-table stability are excluded.
