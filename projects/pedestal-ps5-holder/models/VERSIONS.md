# Version comparison

The mounted holder in the original `pedestal-002.blend` is the source for the new media. Its object is named `Solid_Addition_base_ps5modelv3.001`. The V03 name comes from that source object; it does not establish print approval.

`pedestal_v002.stl` has the same overall dimensions but is not the same mesh. After centring both meshes and aligning their coordinate axes, nearest-surface distances from their vertices reach approximately 4 mm in both directions. Mean distances are approximately 0.208 mm from STL vertices to the scene surface and 0.122 mm in the other direction. This is a vertex-to-surface comparison, not an exhaustive surface tolerance certificate.

`pedestal-v03-bottom-001.stl` is only 4.233 mm thick. `pedestal-v03-measure-bottom.stl` is a partial piece about 49.764 mm high. Neither replaces the complete holder.

The scene export in `candidates/` is the complete rendered geometry. It is a new STL export, not a replacement for any original print file. The archived files remain unchanged. No source was inferred to be physically tested merely from its filename or date.
